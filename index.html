I am a psychologist with more than ten years of professional experience working in public and private healthcare institutions across Argentina. Throughout my career, I have assessed, diagnosed, and treated children, adolescents, adults, couples, families, and groups in a wide variety of clinical and community settings.

My experience spans the three levels of healthcare complexity—from primary care services and specialized addiction centers to a psychiatric teaching hospital—as well as forensic psychology, gender-based violence, autism support, homelessness programs, and institutional network management. Working in these environments strengthened my analytical thinking, attention to detail, evidence-based decision making, collaboration within multidisciplinary teams, and ability to communicate clearly in complex situations.

After building a fulfilling career in psychology, I decided to pursue a new professional challenge in Quality Assurance. I was drawn to QA because it combines many of the skills I have developed throughout my career: investigation, systematic analysis, identifying inconsistencies, documenting findings, and continuously improving quality through collaboration.

I am currently developing my technical skills in software testing through practical projects in manual testing, API testing, and test automation. I have built a GitHub portfolio with hands-on QA projects, I am completing a Quality Assurance diploma at the National University of Córdoba, and I am participating in the Binary Studio Academy Summer Bootcamp.

My goal is to contribute to a collaborative team where I can combine strong analytical thinking, effective communication, and a continuous learning mindset to help deliver high-quality software.
